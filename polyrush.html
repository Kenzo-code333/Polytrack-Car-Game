Power-ups (drive through ❓ boxes)

  • 🚀 Speed Boost — a burst of nitro.
  • 🟤 Mud Splash — throws mud at the racer ahead. If you get hit, mud covers your screen!
  • 🛡️ Shield — blocks one mud splash or oil slick.
  • 🧲 Coin Magnet — pulls nearby coins to you.
  • 🛢️ Oil Slick — drop it behind you to spin out chasers.

Blue arrow pads give a free boost, and yellow ramps launch you into the air. Collect 🪙 coins on the road, then spend them in the garage. Win a race to earn a 🎁 treasure chest with coins, parts or exclusive upgrades!

Race with friends

Online races work when you play from the game's shared claude.ai link. Open that link, then share it with your friends from its Share menu so they can open it too.

Create a race, then send your friends the invite. They open the game link, press Online, and join with the code.
